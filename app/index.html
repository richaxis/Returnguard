SIMPLE. TRANSPARENT. EVIDENCE-LED.

A fairer way to handle a refund.

ReturnGuard organizes the evidence and policy context so disagreements can be evaluated consistently.

01

Start with the policy

Add the product, your claim, the exact policy quote, and a public HTTPS page where the quote can be verified. The quote hash is recorded when you publish.

02

Let the other side respond

A challenge requires an exactly matching bond. Authors cannot challenge their own case, and invalid or unknown case IDs are rejected.

03

Evidence snapshot

During resolution, validators agree on a short excerpt around the policy quote. The excerpt and its hash are stored with the case.

04

Get an outcome

GenLayer consensus evaluates the excerpt against the claim and quoted policy. Unreadable or unclear evidence becomes inconclusive rather than leaving the case stuck.

ⓘ
Important to know

ReturnGuard provides a structured evidence-resolution workflow; it is not a merchant, bank, card issuer, or legal service. A bond is not the purchase price or the refund itself. For a real merchant refund, you may still need to contact the seller or payment provider.
